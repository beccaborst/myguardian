COMMON QUESTIONS

What insurers ask us

How is this different from telematics or catastrophe modeling?
Cat models tell you what might happen. Telematics tells you how people drive. We tell you what protective actions your policyholders actually take before a loss event—and give them the tools to take more proactive behavior. It's behavioral data you can use for pricing, not just forecasting.
What if policyholders don't download the app?
You can incentivize adoption with premium discounts, deductible credits, or priority claims handling. The ROI is positive even at 30% adoption.
How do you handle data privacy and security?
We're SOC 2 Type II compliant with end-to-end encryption. Policyholders opt in explicitly, control what data they share, and can revoke access anytime.
